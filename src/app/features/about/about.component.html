About Us

Welcome to Rainbow Cash & Carry – Your One Stop Online Super Market

At Rainbow Cash & Carry, we believe shopping should be an experience to cherish. Since 2008, we’ve been more than just a store but we’re a community hub where quality, variety, and exceptional service come together under one roof.

Founded in 2008 by Imran Saleemi with a vision to bring world-class retail to Wapda Town Lahore, Rainbow Cash & Carry started as a modest showroom and has grown into multiple cities a premier departmental store.

From humble beginnings serving local families, we’ve evolved into a trusted name synonymous with:
1. Quality Products
2. Competitive Prices
3. Exceptional Customer Service
4. Innovative Shopping Experiences
