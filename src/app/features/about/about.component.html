A MESSAGE FROM OUR CEO

CEO Message

At Rainbow Hypermarket, we have always believed in the power of innovation to shape the future. From our humble beginnings in 2008, we have steadily expanded across Punjab, Pakistan, introducing all products under one roof while offering unmatched customer experiences.

Our mission is simple yet profound: to be the most trusted and innovative retail destination that brings convenience and quality to every corner of the nation. We are not just a hypermarket; we are a symbol of growth, community, and trust. Our journey is one of continuous learning and improvement, and we are excited about the future as we continue to explore new frontiers in retail.

With gratitude,M. Imran Saleemi

01 / THE BEGINNING

Big dreams, humble beginnings.

Born into a humble family in a village, founder Imran Saleemi began working as a rack boy in a departmental store. That experience shaped his ambition to make modern shopping accessible to more people.

02 / THE FIRST STORE

A promise made in Lahore.

In 2008, Imran opened Rainbow in Wapda Town, Lahore. The goal was clear: give everyday shoppers the best value for their money without compromising on quality or service.

03 / TODAY

Growing with the community.

Rainbow now serves communities through 10 stores across Punjab, with more than 2,000 people carrying its original commitment to integrity and care forward.
